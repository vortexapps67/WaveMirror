Privacy Policy

1. Data Storage: WaveMirror operates entirely in-app and in your browser. We do not store or upload your watchlist or movie reviews to external databases. All search histories, watchlist items, and watch party profiles are cached locally on your device via localStorage.

2. Peer-to-Peer Transmission: When hosting or joining a Watch Party, communication data (messages, pause/play synchronization coordinates, voice packets) are relayed directly between participants using WebRTC peer-to-peer connections. No media is stored on our servers.

3. Third-Party Links: Streaming video links are loaded from third-party servers. We recommend keeping the built-in ad & popup shield active to clear any tracking scripts loaded by external player servers.

Terms of Service

1. Acceptable Use: WaveMirror is a free entertainment utility powered by public TMDB APIs and indexers. You agree to use this platform for personal, non-commercial streaming only.

2. Playback Disclaimer: Content feeds and embeds are loaded from free public providers. WaveMirror does not host any video content directly on its local server environment.

3. Watch Party Moderation: Host room creators hold administrative rights. By joining a room, you agree to respect community guidelines. Shared links are to be sent at your own discretion.
